Detox & Immunity

Sweating is the body’s natural and safe way to eliminate toxins. Infrared light amplifies this by heating the body directly on a cellular level, raising core temperature and stimulating the sweat glands — resulting in a deep, detoxifying sweat 7 to 10 times greater than a conventional sauna.

Pain Relief

Infrared wavelengths penetrate deep into muscles, promoting microcirculation and oxygen delivery to stressed or injured areas. This helps down-regulate inflammatory signalling, reduce swelling, and relieve pain — supporting better flexibility and faster recovery from both training load and chronic conditions.

Skin Health

Targeting the epidermis and dermis layers of the skin, infrared sauna and light therapy stimulates collagen production, firms and improves skin tone and elasticity. It has also been proven effective for the treatment of acne, eczema, and psoriasis.

Cardiovascular & Caloric Burn

Studies have shown a single infrared sauna session can burn up to 600 calories by increasing heart rate, cardiac output, and metabolic rate. The intense heat causes blood vessels to dilate, boosting heart rate and strengthening the heart muscle similarly to cardiovascular exercise.

Cognitive Benefits

Regular infrared sauna use significantly lowers dementia and Alzheimer’s risk — with studies showing up to a 66% reduction. It also reduces neuroinflammation, improves sleep quality, and lowers cortisol levels. Hugely beneficial for women going through menopause, through enhanced blood flow and oxygen delivery to the brain.

Hormone Balance

Regular sauna use is associated with a 25% reduction in baseline cortisol levels. By reducing chronic stress and improving circulation, it supports a healthier balance of hormones like oestrogen and progesterone — benefiting overall wellbeing, energy, and vitality.
